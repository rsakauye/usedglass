## Editorial voice

- **Real photos, real gear.** UsedGlass is for people who want to record their own lives and places as they happened: the kid's first hockey game, a larch hike, a wedding, a grandparent's hands. We celebrate photographs that were actually taken.
- **Only real photography.** Every image on the site and in the newsletter is a real photograph: ours, a reader's (with credit and permission), or a dealer or manufacturer product shot used with permission. **No AI-generated imagery, ever,** including headers, thumbnails and "illustrative" pictures. If there's no real photo, we use type.
- **No AI hype.** We don't sell "AI features" as reasons to buy. We describe what a camera does (autofocus tracking, sensor size, weight) in plain words. If a feature is computational, we say what it does, not how magical it is.
- **Buy used, shoot real.** Used gear is the default recommendation. It's cheaper, it keeps working cameras in use, and it's how most of us got started. We recommend new gear when a rebate, a currency gap or a warranty makes it the better buy.
- **Honest numbers.** Every price claim comes from UsedGlass data (price history, medians, deal score) or a cited source. We say "estimate" when it is one. We never invent statistics. When our own data is too thin (history started in October 2026), we say so.
- **Canadian first.** Prices in CAD, landed costs that include shipping, GST/HST and duty, Canadian dealers first, and Canadian seasons, rules and events.
- **Plain, warm, practical.** We write like a friend behind the counter of a good used camera shop: specific gear, specific reasons, no gatekeeping.

**What this means for buyers (our angle):** demand is strong for *new* compacts, instax and film. The *used* market is split. Mass-produced film SLRs and many vintage lenses are plentiful and softening (good time to buy), while cult compacts (Contax T2/T3, Olympus mju-II, Ricoh GR film) are priced as collectibles and carry repair risk. That's a clear, honest story UsedGlass can tell with its own price history.

**Gaps:** We found no Canada-specific survey on AI-image fatigue or film sales. Don't claim Canadian numbers until we have them, for example from our own newsletter poll or UsedGlass listing counts.

### November 2026: Black Friday, post-wedding season, hockey and aurora

- **Seasons:** End of wedding season in Canada; hockey (minor hockey and WHL running since Sept/Oct); aurora season (dark, long nights); Remembrance Day (Nov 11) documentary shooting; Grey Cup (mid-Nov).

Manufacturer instant savings commonly run through November and December (reasoning based on past Canon, Nikon, Sony and Fujifilm Canada holiday programs; confirm on our rebates page before claiming). Canon's fall RF lens cashback ended Oct 29 and the EOS R8 Mark II preorder offer ended Oct 31 (from our rebates data), so expect the holiday program to replace them.
- **Used-market timing:** Wedding photographers finish their season in October and commonly sell or upgrade over the winter (reasoning; we'll watch listing counts for 70-200 f/2.8, 24-70 f/2.8 and pro bodies). New launches like the Canon EOS R8 Mark II (preorders opened Sept 2026, per our rebates data) should push trade-ins of the original R8 and RP into used stock.
- **Analog & Authentic:** Film SLRs make sought-after gifts, and UK auction data shows mass-produced SLRs softening (CameraWorth, Aug 2026), so it's a buyer's market for a Canon AE-1 or Pentax K1000. Warn about cult compacts at collector prices.
