Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

UsedGlass.ca is a small, independent price tracker for used camera gear, run from Alberta, Canada. This page explains in plain language what information we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

The short version: you can use UsedGlass.ca without giving us any personal information. We don't set our own cookies, we don't use advertising trackers, and we never sell your personal information.

Who we are

"UsedGlass.ca", "we" and "us" mean the operator of this website, based in Alberta, Canada.

When you visit the site

  • Analytics. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It doesn't use cookies and doesn't track you across websites. We only see totals, like which pages are popular, which sites sent visitors, and browser, device and country counts. It can't tell us who you are.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare handles your IP address and basic request details to deliver pages and protect the site from attacks. If it needs to check that you're not a bot, Cloudflare may set a short-lived security cookie. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • Search and filters. Searching and filtering listings happens in your own browser. What you type isn't sent to us.
  • Wish lists and My kit. Wish lists, kits and the My kit card are stored only in your browser (its local storage). They're never sent to us, and we can't see them. Share links carry the list inside the link itself; when you open one, the part after the # stays in your browser and isn't sent to our server. Clearing your browser data deletes your lists, so use Export on the My kit page to keep a copy.
  • No accounts. There are no logins, comments or user profiles.

The weekly deals newsletter

If you sign up, we collect your email address and nothing else. The newsletter runs on Kit (formerly ConvertKit), which stores your address, when you signed up, and standard email statistics such as opens and clicks. We use your address only to send the UsedGlass.ca newsletter.

  • We only email you if you've signed up yourself, as Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL) requires.
  • Every email says who it's from and has an unsubscribe link.
  • You can unsubscribe at any time with one click, and we'll stop emailing you right away.

When you submit an event

The event form collects your name and email (so we can ask about the listing) and the event details you type in. Your name and email are never published. Submissions are stored with Cloudflare and checked by a person before anything goes up; only the event details (title, date, venue, city, link and description) can appear on the site. Organizer names and emails are deleted 90 days after the event, automatically. To stop spam, the form uses Cloudflare Turnstile, and we keep a one-way hash of your IP address for one hour to limit repeat submissions.

When you contact us

If you email us, for example to reply to the newsletter or to ask for a listing correction as a dealer, we keep your email address and message so we can answer you. The site has no other forms besides the newsletter sign-up.

Affiliate links and retailer cookies

Some links on UsedGlass.ca are affiliate links, such as the "Check new price on Amazon.ca" links on model pages. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. We may add other affiliate partners in the future, such as camera retailers, and the same rules will apply.

When you click an affiliate link, the retailer or its affiliate network may set cookies on its own site so it knows you came from UsedGlass.ca and can credit us for a purchase. We don't set those cookies, and the retailer's own privacy policy covers them. We only get summary reports, such as how many clicks and qualifying sales a link produced. We never see your name, address or payment details. You can block or clear these cookies in your browser settings. Links to dealer listings are not affiliate links.

We don't sell your information

We never sell, rent or trade personal information. We only share it with the services that run the site (Cloudflare for hosting and analytics, Kit for the newsletter), or when the law requires it.

Where it's stored and for how long

Cloudflare and Kit are based in the United States, so your information may be stored or processed outside Canada and may be subject to the laws there. We keep your newsletter address until you unsubscribe, and emails only as long as we need them to help you.

Your rights

Canada's privacy law for businesses, the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), applies to UsedGlass.ca, along with Alberta's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) where it applies. You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, have it corrected, or have it deleted. If you're not happy with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Contact

Questions, requests or concerns? Email us at hello@usedglass.ca, or write to UsedGlass.ca, PO Box 1604, Okotoks, AB T1S 1B5, Canada.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
