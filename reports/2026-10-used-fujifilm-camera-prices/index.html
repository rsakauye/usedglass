How we made this report

  • Data window: Oct 17, 2026 to Oct 30, 2026 (14 daily checks).
  • Sources: the used listings on each dealer's own website, checked once a day: Camera Canada, Camera Traders, Downtown Camera, Gosselin Photo, Maple Camera Co., McBain Camera, Plot Camera, The Camera Store, eBay (Canadian sellers).
  • Prices are in Canadian dollars, before tax and shipping, as the dealer listed them.
  • Bodies only. Kits with a lens are included under the body model, which can raise the high price.
  • We leave out listings that look mismatched: lenses or accessories filed under a body name, anything under $150, and anything under 30% of the model's median.
  • We started tracking on October 17, 2026, so there's no month-over-month comparison yet.
