Rate moved, prices haven't

CAD moved 0.2% against the USD over 30 days, under the 2.5% threshold, so no price flags today.

How it works: when the Canadian dollar moves more than 2.5% against the US dollar over 30 days, we list Canadian new-gear prices (from 883 models we track at The Camera Store) that haven't changed in that time. They may adjust: dealers often reprice when new stock arrives at the new exchange rate, but many hold prices, and manufacturers set Canadian MAP prices on their own schedule. We need at least two weeks of our own price history before listing anything (26 days so far).
