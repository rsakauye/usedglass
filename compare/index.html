Compare camera bodies like RPG gear

Pick up to three bodies. Six stats on the radar, each scored against the best of the 16 bodies here (100 = best in this list). Green + buffs and red − debuffs come from each maker's own specs, with a source on every line.

    What the stats mean

    • RES resolution: effective megapixels.
    • SPD speed: fastest full-resolution, uncropped burst on either shutter.
    • VID video: widest internal recording plus top 4K frame rate.
    • STA stamina: CIPA shots per charge using the rear screen.
    • AGI agility: lighter scores higher (weight with battery and card).
    • STB stabilisation: maker's best in-body claim in stops.

    Stats are spec-sheet numbers, not lab tests. Lens choice, firmware and how you shoot matter more than any one number.
